## The decision rule (written now, applied mechanically by score_w9.py)
Anchors are the recounted P-a counts: W8 **2**, W6 **10**. With k = W9's P-a count:
- **|k − 2| ≤ 3 (k ≤ 5) → "the computation carries it".**
- **k ≥ 7 (within 3 of W6's 10, or higher) → "order and claims carry it".**
- **otherwise (k = 6) → "both contribute".**

Said now so it is not discovered later: (i) with these anchors the middle band is a single value, k = 6; (ii) k = 7 or 8 is both "P-a holds" and "order and claims carry it": the arm would then catch cheap talk well enough by the old band while still sitting closer to W6 than to W8. Both readings are reported, neither overrides the other. (iii) Beside the rule, not changing it: if W9's **P-b is falsified**, a low k comes from doubting everything, not from the computation, and the verdict is reported with that next to it. (iv) Temperature 0 is not a replication: W6 and W8 are single samples, so a difference of a few runs is within what a re-sample could move; the rule's ±3 is the only allowance made.

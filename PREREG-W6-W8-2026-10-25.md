## A caveat written now, not after
The do-label is the end price (last-7 mean) measured against this same ruler. A partner that reads the ruler well is recovering the label's own rule, and W8's part 1 asks for the last-7 average explicitly, so W8 is the closer of the two to executing that rule. The paper says so and puts beside it: **the rule itself (100% by construction)** and **the price correlation, no ruler (AUROC 0.855)**. What the arms can show beyond the rule is the part the rule cannot do: whether a reader handed the ruler *uses* it against an anchored first answer (P-a), keeps what is right (P-b), and uses the board fact against invented talk (the 9).
